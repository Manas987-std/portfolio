I'm Muhammad Anus Javed, a 5th-semester Computer Science student at the University of Sargodha. Most recently I've been teaching myself game development — working through how a game actually gets built, from core mechanics to a playable loop, rather than just reading theory about it.

Alongside that, I build practical web systems as part of my coursework. My main project so far is a Complaint Management System that lets a citizen file a complaint and routes it to the right institution until it's resolved — the kind of workflow that's easy to describe and genuinely hard to get right.

I like projects with a clear before-and-after: an unplayable game becomes a playable one, an unresolved complaint becomes a resolved one.
